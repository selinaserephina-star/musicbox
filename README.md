# 🎛️ SoundBox

A dead-simple, browser-based electronic music studio — built to be *playable* rather than complicated. One self-contained HTML file, no install, no accounts, nothing uploaded: it all runs on your machine.

Designed around the **Novation Launchkey Mini**, but works great with just a mouse and your computer keyboard too.

## ▶️ How to run

**Open `SoundBox.html` in Chrome or Edge** (double-click, or right-click → Open with). That's it.

> Use Chrome/Edge — they support the Web MIDI API needed for the Launchkey. Allow MIDI/mic access when asked.

## ✨ What it does

- **73 built-in instruments** — pianos, guitars, strings, mallets, winds, basses, pads, leads and synths — all synthesized (no samples), grouped by category.
- **16-pad drum machine** with a step sequencer, multiple **kits** (Punchy / Chill / Deep), and switchable **1/16 ↔ 1/32 timing**.
- **Loops & layers** — record what you play into looping layers (1–8 bars), each with its own **instrument, speed (½×/2×) and mute**.
- **Parts + Song builder** — build sections (verse, drop, 🌉 one-click **Bridge** in a related key) and arrange them into a full track; each part keeps its own **key/scale and tempo**.
- **Timeline** — record audio clips, drag them in time (snap to bar / beat / ½-beat), build sections, and **export the whole thing to WAV**.
- **Chords & Scales helper** — pick a key/scale, see the mood, the chords that fit, borrowed chords, and related keys; click progressions to hear them or drop them in as a loop. In-key notes glow on the on-screen piano.
- **Generative helpers** — ✨ Suggest beat / bassline / melody, all in your current key.
- **Effects & live dashboard** — distortion, chorus, reverb, delay; a real-time spectrum graph and a live master filter you can sweep, with a movement graph.
- **Mic input** — sing/talk over your beat, or hum a melody and have it transcribed into editable notes.
- **Save / open** your whole song as a small `.sbx` project file.

## 🎹 Launchkey Mini

Keys play the current synth; pads trigger drums; knobs map to filter/reverb/delay (with MIDI-Learn). Not sure of your model? The built-in **MIDI monitor** shows exactly what your device sends so you can pick the right pad-input mode.
